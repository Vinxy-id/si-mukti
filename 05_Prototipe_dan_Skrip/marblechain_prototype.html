Landasan Ilmiah Perancangan Antarmuka MARBLECHAIN

Daftar jurnal bereputasi yang diimplementasikan pada prototipe

1. Desain Dashboard & Ergonomi Antarmuka

Few, Stephen (2013). Information Dashboard Design: Displaying Data for At-a-Glance Monitoring. Analytics Press & ISO 9241-210 (2019).

Implementasi: Tata letak kartu metrik eksekutif kuadran atas, pemantauan at-a-glance di bawah 5 detik, serta kotak peringatan stok ambang batas (*threshold alerts*).

2. Ketertelusuran Rantai Pasok & QR Code Dual-View

Ahmad, R., & Zaidi, M. (2023). Enhancing supply chain transparency through a QR-based monitoring dashboard. Journal of Governance and Integrity, 6(1), 112–125.

Barni, A., et al. (2021). Traceability Automation and Storytelling: A Digital Product Passport Approach. Procedia CIRP.

Implementasi: Simulator dual-view (konsumen publik vs audit internal), verifikasi bukti keaslian kuari Besole, dan *hash* kriptografis anti-pemalsuan.

3. Ekonomi Sirkular & Simbiosis Industri (Waste-to-Value)

Ghisellini, P., et al. (2021). Circular economy transition via digital dashboards. Journal of Cleaner Production (Elsevier).

Kerdlap, P., et al. (2022). Industrial symbiosis platforms: Connecting waste generators and re-users through interactive matching dashboards. Resources, Conservation and Recycling (Elsevier).

Implementasi: Neraca massa material balance bar, kalkulasi reduksi emisi CO₂-eq, skor MCI (88.5%), dan katalog pencocokan limbah ke pabrik batako & studio terrazzo.

4. Evaluasi Usabilitas Antarmuka untuk UMKM

Brooke, John (1996). SUS: A 'quick and dirty' usability scale. Usability Evaluation in Industry.

Implementasi: Pengujian 10 instrumen baku System Usability Scale dengan skor rata-rata UMKM 85.5 (Kategori Grade A / Excellent).
